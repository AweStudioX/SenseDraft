# Manual test pass

1. Launch `build/SenseDraft.app`. In the first-launch Permissions page, grant Accessibility access in macOS System Settings and click **Check again**. If macOS does not recognize the app after rebuilding, remove the old SenseDraft entry and add the current app bundle again. Reopen Settings and confirm the Home page displays the app logo; use the sidebar to open AI & Writing, Shortcuts, and Permissions. Check the sidebar and content in both light and dark appearance.
2. In AI & Writing, choose **API Key**, enter a valid OpenAI key, choose a model and English locale, and click **Save AI settings**. Keep the ChatGPT plan sign-in test separate; it requires an eligible account and browser authorization.
3. In Shortcuts, record Analyze and Replace, save them, relaunch, and confirm they persisted. Click **Restore defaults** and confirm `⌥⌘E` and `⌥⌘Return` work.
   With Settings focused, verify Escape and ⌘W each close the window. While a shortcut box is recording, verify Escape cancels recording without closing Settings; verify ⌘W closes Settings and the global shortcuts still work afterward.
4. In TextEdit plain text mode, type an awkward English sentence. Keep the cursor in that field and press Analyze without selecting text. Confirm the whole field appears in the panel, with improved English and Chinese meaning; typing alone should not open the panel or send a request.
5. Edit the Chinese meaning and click **Regenerate from Chinese**. Confirm the candidate updates while TextEdit remains unchanged. Press Replace and verify the complete field changes once.
6. Analyze again, then change the original text or focus a different external field before Replace. Confirm replacement is refused. Press Escape to dismiss the panel and confirm the source stays unchanged.
7. Repeat the read, copy, and replace checks in Sublime Text, Safari and Chrome textareas, contenteditable editors, and your main messaging editor. In Sublime, start with an unsaved disposable document: Analyze without preselecting text, confirm the entire text appears, and review the result. With **Allow Replace to remove text formatting** off in Settings → Home, confirm Replace is blocked for an editor flagged as formatted. Turn the setting on, relaunch, refocus the same editor, then press Replace. Confirm the text changes exactly once without another formatting confirmation. Record observed support in `compatibility.md`.
   In Feishu, use a fresh unsent draft after removing any text appended by an older SenseDraft build. Analyze and confirm an ordinary plain draft does not require a formatting confirmation. Click Replace in the review panel; confirm the panel hides while SenseDraft refocuses the same composer, then the original is gone and the suggestion appears exactly once. Repeat by returning focus to the composer and pressing the Replace shortcut. Do not click Send.
8. For the keyboard fallback, place a distinctive text and image on the clipboard before Analyze, then verify both clipboard representations are restored afterward. Repeat after Replace. Change the source text or switch windows before Replace and confirm it refuses to write. Check the caret position after Analyze; when no Accessibility selection range exists, it may move to the end. Clipboard-history apps may keep the temporary copy.
9. Test an already natural English sentence, Chinese input, mixed Chinese and English input, an empty field, and a password field. Confirm Chinese input produces natural English plus a Chinese meaning check. Confirm no unwanted replacement occurs. Check the rich text warning when the source has mixed formatting. Verify the review panel avoids a small focused input; drag the panel, then analyze again and confirm it reopens at the dragged position. Resize the panel taller and shorter; confirm the English and Chinese editors use the added height while the action buttons remain visible.
10. If you use ChatGPT plan, select it in AI settings, authorize in your browser, then check the account and status in SenseDraft. Choose an available model, save, and run one analysis. The provider's streaming request and parser passed a live API call on 2026-10-12; confirm that the packaged panel now displays both English and Chinese. If the browser shows **Invalid authorization step**, record the diagnostics on that page. If SenseDraft shows a token-exchange HTTP error, record its OAuth error code. Also test **Cancel sign-in**, **Refresh models**, and **Disconnect**.

For a replacement that hides and reopens the review panel, inspect the recent unified logs with:

```sh
/usr/bin/log show --last 10m --style compact --predicate 'subsystem == "com.kaaaaai.SenseDraft" && category == "replacement"'
```

Each attempt has a short ID and stage names. These logs omit editor text, clipboard contents, window titles, prompts, and account data. Compare the first and second attempt IDs to locate the failing stage. The panel reappears when the replacement throws; it does not prove that a paste was sent.
