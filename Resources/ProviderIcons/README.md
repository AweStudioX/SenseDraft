# Provider icons

These icons identify third-party AI services inside SenseDraft. They are resized copies of assets published by their respective providers; SenseDraft does not claim ownership or endorsement.

- `openai-light.png` and `openai-dark.png`: OpenAI's 2025 monochrome Blossom, from [OpenAI's brand assets](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip).
- `deepseek.png`: the whale mark from [DeepSeek's official deepseek-harness repository](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/apps/web/public/favicon.svg), recolored blue for visibility in both appearances.
- `kimi.png`: the rounded icon from [KIMI's official brand guide](https://moonshotai.github.io/Branding-Guide/).
- `claude.png`: Claude's app icon from the installed [Claude for Mac](https://claude.com/download) bundle.
- `gemini.png`: Google's Gemini spark mark, [extracted from the About Gemini page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Gemini_icon_2025.svg).
- `openrouter-light.png` and `openrouter-dark.png`: OpenRouter's grape and volt glyphs from its [official brand assets](https://openrouter.ai/brand/), selected for light and dark appearance respectively.
- `qwen.png`: the [Qwen official site](https://qwenlm.github.io/favicon.png) favicon.
- `ollama.png`: the mark from [Ollama's official repository](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/main/docs/ollama-logo.svg), displayed on white so it remains legible in both appearances.

Each PNG is displayed at its original aspect ratio in the settings and review views.
